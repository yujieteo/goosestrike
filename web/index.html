      Control experiment: goose vs. infinitely rigid wall
      
    
      Fire the goose at a rigid wall, and compare the pressure at the impact centre
      with Wilbeck's two hydrodynamic pressures: the Hugoniot shock pressure
      PH = ρ0Usv at first contact, and the
      stagnation pressure Ps = ½ρ0v² in steady flow.
      
    
    
      
    
  
      

  
      
    
      Receipts
      
    
      
      The rule
      
      “(e) Damage-tolerance (discrete source) evaluation. The airplane must be capable of
        successfully completing a flight during which likely structural damage occurs as a result of—
        (1) Impact with a 4-pound bird when the velocity of the airplane relative to the bird along
        the airplane's flight path is equal to VC at sea level or 0.85VC at 8,000 feet,
        whichever is more critical;”
      
      
      14 CFR 25.571(e)(1). Bird mass 4 lb = 1.814 kg. VC = 350 KCAS,
        the lowest value that 25.1505 allows for an aeroplane with VMO = 350 kt
        (A320 family, EASA TCDS EASA.A.064). At sea level ISA, 350 KCAS = 350 KTAS = 180.06 m/s.
      
    
      
    
      
      What is physics and what is costume
      
      The physics bird is the standard substitute bird: a cylinder with hemispherical ends,
        length/diameter 2, of gelatin with 10 % porosity (950 kg/m³), with a Tait equation
        of state for water (c0 = 1480 m/s, γ = 7). The particles carry the physics.
        The feathers, eyes, beak, wings and blush are paint on those particles. They have no mass.
      
      
      The approach flight before contact is a cartoon. The simulation starts 2 mm before contact.
      
    
      
    
      
      Panel and method
      
      Curved panel, radius 1.975 m, 1.067 m × 0.760 m, clamped edges. Skin 2024-T3,
        1.6 mm. Four Z stringers and two Z frames, 7075-T6. The geometry is a representative
        assumption, not a drawing of a real aeroplane. Normal impact at mid-bay.
      
      
      Bird: SPH. Panel: Belytschko-Lin-Tsay shells, J2 plasticity, erosion at the failure
        plastic strain. Contact: penalty. Central differences with a stable time step.
        All sources and the convergence study are in the
        README.
      
    
      
    
      This is not a certification analysis. Residual strength after the strike is
      not analysed. No real geese were harmed. Several virtual geese were.
